# Fictional student stories

These stories were defined before the seed records. Every person, record and policy is invented for the demonstration.

- **Aanya Kapoor**, second-year Computer Science: a drop in attendance, a hostel concern and a counselling referral form one connected story. An unanswered scholarship query leads to a fee reminder while her referral is still pending. Staff need to coordinate support, pause reminders and accept the handoff.
- **Ishita Rao**, third-year Electronics: her scholarship is five weeks late, she missed one exam and her hostel check-in is quiet. No single department has raised an urgent alert; the cross-department detector connects the signals.
- **Riya Sethi**, first-year IT: she asked for a first counselling appointment 21 days ago and is still waiting for a booking.
- **Diya Nair**, second-year Mechanical: a Health Centre referral to Counselling has been waiting for acceptance for 12 days.
- **Prisha Anand**, first-year Architecture: one family emergency has brought her to Fees, Hostel and Academics. A shared brief saves her from repeating her story.
- **Kavya Bedi**, third-year Computer Science: Health Centre and Academics separately recorded the same medical leave. Linking the records preserves both departmental histories.
- **Meghna Suri**, second-year Electronics: an approved accessibility accommodation has not reached the Exams team. A tracked handoff closes the gap.
- **Tanvi Arora**, fourth-year IT: fee dues and a scholarship enquiry are being coordinated by Student Affairs. Her placement information is available as a department-specific record, and her needs are moderate.
- **Neha Vora**, second-year Architecture: a grievance is restricted to the Grievance team. Other teams should see no case content or event.
- **Simran Khanna**, third-year Mechanical: a fees and academic-support journey was resolved on time. She is the clean control case.
